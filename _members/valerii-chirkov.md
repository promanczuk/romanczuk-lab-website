Valerii is a PhD candidate at Humboldt University of Berlin and the Science of Intelligence Cluster. His research focuses on how human groups search and adapt together in changing environments, particularly when and how people use social information.

His work combines field data, experiments, and modeling: studying foragers in large ice-fishing competitions, running immersive 3D multiplayer search experiments, and simulating collective foraging with multi-agent reinforcement learning and agent-based models. Valerii also employs advanced statistical and machine learning methods, such as simulation-based inference, to fit complex social simulations to behavioral data.

Before joining the lab in 2023, Valerii completed an MSc in neuroscience at the Berlin School of Mind and Brain.
